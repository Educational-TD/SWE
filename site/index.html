Geometry Defense

Can't play? Watch a demo video instead!

Geometry Defense is a tower defense game about defending against various shapes. The purpose is to help middle-schoolers learn the foundational math that they will use in their day-to-day life. The game accomplishes this by having towers only upgradeable by answering a age-appropriate math problems, with higher level towers more capable of defeating the enemy shapes. As students understand the material better, they will be able to survive more waves for a better score, encouraging them to learn the material until it becomes instinct.
